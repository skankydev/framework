# 01 Philosophy

## Who am I

I'm a jack-of-all-trades developer, I started at 13, and my career has been a bit chaotic.
I like to understand the tools I use,
so I rewrite the features I don't understand.

And from one side project to the next, I built SkankyDev.

I think it can be a good start to understand how a framework works.


## Wet kisses are the best

The W.E.T principle is a good one:
an idea is clearer once it's been done several times, and it's easier to generalize when the need shows up.
D.R.Y is nice, but it asks you to plan for things that may never happen, and so you tend to make things too complicated for not much.

Which brings us to K.I.S.S, the most often forgotten one: always aim to make something simple. Of course, that's the hardest part, but
if it's complicated, break it down into smaller problems and everything should get simpler.
